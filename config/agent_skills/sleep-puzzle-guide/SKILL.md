# Sleep Puzzle guide

Use this skill when someone wants to explore Sleep Puzzle's services, find public
articles, or understand how to access their purchases.

## Choose the language

The official website is https://sleeppuzzle.com. Polish pages use the bare path;
English pages use `/en` before the same path. Follow the person's preferred
language. Some English content may fall back to Polish.

## Find the right page

- Consultations and current packages: https://sleeppuzzle.com/packages
- Audio programmes: https://sleeppuzzle.com/audioprocesses
- Bedtime stories: https://sleeppuzzle.com/stories
- Public articles: https://sleeppuzzle.com/blog
- About the consultant: https://sleeppuzzle.com/about
- Contact: https://sleeppuzzle.com/contact
- Purchased audio and bookings (sign-in required): https://sleeppuzzle.com/dashboard
- Terms: https://sleeppuzzle.com/terms
- Refund policy: https://sleeppuzzle.com/refunds
- Privacy: https://sleeppuzzle.com/privacy

Fetch https://sleeppuzzle.com/llms.txt for an index of public pages and articles,
https://sleeppuzzle.com/llms-full.txt for article text, or
https://sleeppuzzle.com/sitemap.xml for published URLs.

## Help the person take the next step

1. Identify whether they want a consultation, an audio programme, a bedtime story,
   an article, or help with an existing purchase.
2. Read the relevant live page and summarize its current information. Link to
   the page so the person can review the details.
3. Read prices, package contents, availability, and purchase conditions from the
   live site. If information is unavailable, say so; do not invent a price or slot.
4. Direct account holders to the dashboard for purchased audio and bookings.
   Account access, checkout, and payment use the site's normal sign-in and Paddle
   flows. Ask the person to complete any required authentication themselves.
5. Before submitting a contact form, making a booking, or purchasing anything,
   obtain the person's explicit authorization for that action.

Describe the site's services and published information without making diagnoses
or promising sleep outcomes. Use the current refund policy and contact page for
purchase questions rather than inventing eligibility rules.
