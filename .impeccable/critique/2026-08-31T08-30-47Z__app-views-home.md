## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The token layer is unmistakably this product. The composition is a template.

Skeleton: hero with two CTAs + illustration → thin stat strip → about with square image left → three-up card grid → numbered process row → split section with tile grid → newsletter capture. Canonical coach/SaaS landing page in canonical order. Swap the mascot for a product screenshot and "Umów konsultację" for "Start free trial" and this ships for a B2B analytics tool without a structural edit.

Expensive misses:
- The puzzle is nowhere in the composition. Product name and declared design north star; only thing carrying it is a 16px border-radius.
- Karola is the product, but her photo is md:w-1/4 in section three, optional, dashed empty box when absent. Hero carries mascot-grey.png (desaturated) while mascot-coral.png and mascot-peach.png sit unused.
- The 3-up symmetric package grid reads as pricing tiers - the visual argument for "one size fits some of you", the opposite of the stated belief.
- "20+ lat" is the only real evidence and it is spent on a full-width band containing one chip.

**Deterministic scan:** detector ran twice (`app/views/home`; then `app/views/shared app/views/newsletter_subscriptions app/components`). Both exited 0 with ZERO findings.

That clean result is the most interesting datum: the detector found nothing while the review found two WCAG Level A failures. The defects live behind ViewComponent indirection - the detector cannot see that Section::Component renders its title as a `<span>`, or that the hamburger's only child is an aria-hidden SVG. A clean detector run on a ViewComponent codebase is not evidence of accessibility.

Static grep sweep independently confirmed both P0s: no `<h1>` anywhere, and `image_tag "mascot-grey.png"` at index.html.erb:20 with no alt: argument.

**False positive discarded:** sweep flagged `bg-white dark:bg-neutral-900 text-neutral-500` in `app/components/forms/component.html.erb:30` as rendering into the home page. It does not - that is the FLOATING label variant; the newsletter form passes `label_hidden: true`, taking the default branch which renders a proper `sr-only` label. Latent trap for a future `variant: :floating` form, not a live defect.

**Visual overlays:** none. No dev server running; booting Rails needs PostgreSQL + env. No overlay exists.

## Overall Impression

The surface has been held in a real hand - comments record genuine device-level fixes (overflow-x-clip for a glow overflowing a phone viewport, flex-wrap because two nowrap pills exceed a phone width, flex-1 steps so a fourth step divides rather than breaks). Craft in the details is well above average.

The problem is one level up: the design system is doing all the identity work and the layout is doing none. A distinctive visual world laid over a stock composition. Underneath that, two Level A accessibility failures on the one page that receives Instagram traffic, on a project where WCAG 2.1 AA is legally binding.

Biggest opportunity: the page never lets the visitor meet Karola, and never gives her anything to do that costs less than a booking.

## What's Working

1. **Content-block discipline is complete and is the right product decision.** Every visible word except chrome resolves through content_block / content_items with a shipped default. Includes the split where the label is CMS copy and the arrow glyph is markup, so rewording cannot destroy the chrome.
2. **Absent-rather-than-empty is genuinely implemented.** index.html.erb:76 renders a dashed frame instead of a broken img; :110 hides the packages grid rather than showing an empty comb.
3. **"Never trade on fear" is honoured completely.** No countdowns, scarcity, implied parental failure, autoplay, third-party request or cookie banner.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] No `<h1>` on the page - the hero title is a `<span>`
`app/components/section/component.html.erb:7` renders the title as `<span class="text-t1 font-display font-bold text-cream">`. Home has zero h1 and jumps to five h2s. Every other page does this correctly (products/index.html.erb:12, terms/show.html.erb:11, bookings/index.html.erb:3).
**Why:** WCAG 2.1 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (Level A), binding under the EAA. Screen-reader user from Instagram gets no page title and no heading skim. Costs the highest-traffic page its SEO anchor.
**Fix:** add `heading_level:` to Section::Component (default :h2), render `content_tag(heading_level, title, class: "text-t1 font-display font-bold text-cream")`, pass `heading_level: :h1` from index.html.erb:11. Zero visual change.
**Command:** /impeccable audit app/views/home

### [P0] The hamburger button has no accessible name
`app/components/navbar/component.html.erb:29` renders a button whose only content is `hamburger_icon` → `helpers.icon("menu", size: 18)`; ApplicationHelper::ICON_ATTRS sets `aria-hidden="true"` on every icon. No aria-label, no sr-only text.
**Why:** WCAG 2.1 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (Level A). Below lg this is the ONLY route to navigation, cart, sign-in and the booking CTA - and mobile is the stated design target. Screen reader announces "button". Task not completable.
**Fix:** add `aria-label="<%= t("nav.menu") %>"` to the trigger and a `nav.menu` key to both locale files.
**Command:** /impeccable audit app/views/shared

### [P1] Hero secondary CTA effectively invisible; mascot has no alt
index.html.erb:40 overrides to `bg-ink-soft! border-taupe-dark!` on an ink section. Measured: fill #26221f on #211e1c = 1.05:1; border #57504a = 2.09:1. System default border-input is only 1.65:1. WCAG 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast requires 3:1. Separately index.html.erb:20 `image_tag "mascot-grey.png"` has no alt attribute - fails 1.1.1 (Level A). Footer does it correctly (_footer.html.erb:15).
**Why:** "Zobacz pakiety" is the low-commitment option a frightened parent most wants before booking, and it is the one she cannot see at 20% brightness at 3am.
**Fix:** `alt: ""` on the mascot. Drop the button override; raise outline to border-tan (#cfc7bc = 9.9:1) or fill bg-surface with border-border-input.
**Command:** /impeccable polish app/views/home

### [P1] The four product tiles are dead
index.html.erb:190-202 renders each Product in a Card::Component with no link_to, no href, no action - beneath a package grid whose cards ARE navigable, same radius, border and amber title.
**Why:** strongest visual signal is "same kind of thing as the cards above". Tired one-handed reader taps, gets nothing, no feedback. Dead-affordance failure four times in a row.
**Fix:** wrap each tile in `link_to product_path(product)`; give it the affordance the packages carry.
**Command:** /impeccable clarify app/views/home

### [P1] The page ends on an ask
Final section (:217-228) is newsletter capture; last element before the footer is an empty email input.
**Why:** peak-end. Residual impression of a brand positioned on warmth and access to a person is that it wanted her email. Asked three times (book, book, subscribe), reassured once, in a section she may never have reached. The emotional peak - the about section, Karola's actual voice - is buried at position three below the fold with an optional photo.
**Fix:** move the newsletter above the audio strip or into the footer band; close on the human - Karola's photo, the "Piasek jest fajny" quote, and one low-stakes link "Nie wiesz od czego zacząć? Napisz do mnie" → contact_path.
**Command:** /impeccable layout app/views/home

### Also worth fixing
**[P2] Three amber pills in one viewport.** _navbar.html.erb:154 and :162 force bg-accent! onto variant: :secondary buttons; hero adds a third. Violates the One Lamp Rule. Let "Zaloguj się" be a real secondary.
**[P2] Copyright line fails contrast.** _footer.html.erb:57 sets text-taupe-dark (#57504a) on surface-dark (#1a1817) = 2.23:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement. DESIGN.md already forbids this. text-taupe gives 6.0:1.

## Minor Observations

- `dark:text-cream` at :117 and :195 - dead code, forbidden by DESIGN.md.
- 19 `!` important-overrides in this one file. Symptom, not decision: Card::Component's default border is `border-black/10 dark:border-white/10`, so every call site must write `border-border-strong!`.
- Buttons::Component emits `role="buttons"` on anchors and defaults `type: "buttons"`. Invalid role string is ignored; `type="buttons"` falls back to submit per spec. Neither misfires here - lucky, not correct.
- Section::Component silently discards `classes:` (assigned, never used in wrapper_classes) and `bordered:` (border_classes derives from @background). Six sections pass `bordered: false` for no effect.
- About CTA resolves through content_link_url → safe_link_url, which returns `/about` unchanged because it starts with `/`. Drops the locale, so on /en it lands on the Polish about page. The navbar logo has a comment about fixing precisely this bug.
- No prefers-reduced-motion handling anywhere in the sweep. Low impact today; a gap the moment anything is animated.
- The astroid - the design system's "cheapest signature" - appears exactly once on this page, in the section that has one item in it.
- Alternating band rule followed correctly end to end. Credit.
